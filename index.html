AAC today is expensive, hard to learn, and slow. Language tools have changed faster than AAC has. We are starting LingoLinq from that gap, and we are looking for AAC users, families, SLPs, and administrators to help us think through what comes next.

Why now.

AAC communicators average 12 to 15 words a minute. Speakers average 125 to 150. Cost, learning curve, and limited language depth are the rest of the gap. Language tools have changed faster than AAC ever has. We think there is a window to do something about it, with the people who live with AAC every day in the room while it gets built.

Privacy is the line.

Ethical AI in AAC means no training on user communication, no sale of vocabulary patterns, and no quiet data harvesting. The hard question is how to use modern language tools to remove the speed, cost, and learning-curve barriers in AAC without crossing that line.

We do not have every answer yet. We have principles, and we are looking for AAC users, SLPs, families, and administrators to help us find the rest.

What we will not promise.

LingoLinq is pre-launch. We will say plainly what is real and what is roadmap.

  • A finished product. We are pre-launch and we will keep saying so until that changes.
  • Compliance certifications. We are designing toward FERPA, HIPAA, and GDPR. We have not been audited.
  • Specific AI features. We are exploring what ethical AI can do for AAC. We will say what is real when it is real.
  • Open source. The source and roadmap live on GitHub.
